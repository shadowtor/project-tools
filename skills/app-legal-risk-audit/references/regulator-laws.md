# Regulator and portal laws (11 items)

Source: https://www.murphmaxxing.com/vault/10-ways-your-app-is-breaking-the-law - verify current law, dates and penalties there (the article lists more than ten; the last one concerns the EU Cyber Resilience Act).

Unlike the "sued" lists, these have a regulator and a form, and some have public reporting portals. Many are region-specific: note which regions the app serves and mark items outside them "not applicable" with the reason.

| # | Risk | Region/law | How to check in code | Fails when | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Images with no licence | US copyright | Images in the repo/`public`, `assets`; licence files or source notes | Stock-looking or scraped images with no licence record | Licence file or source link next to each image; delete what can't be sourced |
| 2 | Cookie banner: reject harder than accept | GDPR/ePrivacy | Consent component; Network tab in a private window | Reject is smaller, deeper or slower than accept, or non-essential scripts load before consent | Reject = same size and clicks as accept; nothing non-essential loads before accept |
| 3 | User content with no report route | EU DSA Art. 16 | Posts, comments, reviews, uploads; report/flag UI and endpoint; legal contact page | UGC with no report button, no queue anyone reads, no contact point, no moderation paragraph in terms | Report button on every piece of UGC to a real queue; contact email on `/legal`; terms paragraph on removal and appeal |
| 4 | Health or precise-location data sent to analytics | US state health-data laws (e.g. Washington) | Analytics SDK init and event calls; properties being sent | Symptom, mood, cycle, fitness or precise-location fields leave the app in events | Allowlist of event properties; strip anything health-shaped; separate consent screen; published consumer health data policy |
| 5 | UGC service with no risk assessment | UK Online Safety Act | Presence of UK users and user-to-user posting; docs folder | No written illegal-content risk assessment | Write it (Ofcom publishes a small-service template); ensure the report button exists |
| 6 | Fake urgency | UK DMCC, US deceptive practices | Countdown timers, "only N left", "N people viewing" | Timer restarts on refresh, hard-coded or `Math.random()` stock/viewer numbers | Back every urgency number with a real DB value and real end time; delete the rest |
| 7 | AI described as therapy/counselling | Illinois HB 1806 and similar | System prompts, marketing copy, store listings for "therapy/therapist/counseling/treatment" | Product presented as therapy with no licensed human involved | Remove those words; call it journaling or coaching and mean it; geo-block or add a licensed clinician; keep a self-harm protocol |
| 8 | Per-user pricing without a label | NY Algorithmic Pricing Disclosure Act | Pricing logic that varies by user history, device or location | Price depends on a per-user signal and no disclosure string is shown beside it | Render the required disclosure beside such prices, or make prices uniform and use discount codes |
| 9 | Image/video upload with no takedown SLA | US TAKE IT DOWN Act | Upload and AI-image features; a notice-and-removal route | Only a support email exists; no 48-hour SLA; no hash to block re-uploads | `/report-image` route taking link, signature and non-consent statement; ticket with 48h SLA; hash file to block re-uploads |
| 10 | AI output not labelled | EU AI Act Art. 50 | Chat UI copy; image/voice generation output metadata | Chat never says it's AI; generated files carry no machine-readable mark; voice/face likeness unlabeled | Persistent "you're talking to an AI" line; C2PA or equivalent metadata on generated files; visible label on realistic people |
| 11 | No security contact or vulnerability intake | EU Cyber Resilience Act | Mobile/downloadable builds; `security.txt`; error monitoring; dependency alerts | Distributed app with no security contact, no intake, no runbook for the 24h/72h/14d reports | `security.txt` with a monitored address; error monitoring that pages; dependency alerts; one-page reporting runbook (pure SaaS is mostly outside scope) |
